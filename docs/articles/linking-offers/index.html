Understanding Offer Linking

What is Offer Linking?

Offer linking is the process of activating your cashback offers to a specific debit card, credit card, or grocery store account before you check out. This ensures that your purchases are eligible for cashback rewards.

Why is Linking Important?

  • Ensures eligibility: Purchases made without activated offers are not eligible for cashback rewards or discounts.
  • Maximizes earnings: You can stack linked cashback offers with other cashback programs, such as Rakuten (for online shopping through a link) and Fetch (for receipt scanning), to get a higher cashback rate.
  • Receive reminders: Cashback program providers usually send reminder emails before an offer expires, so you don’t miss out on any rewards.

How GetCashback.ai Links Offers

Browser Extension Widget

When you visit a cashback offer provider, GetCashback.ai:

  1. Displays the Widget to guide you through the process.
  2. Automatically detects offers on the offer page.
  3. One click to start collecting — the Widget will go through offers one by one to link all of them to the account or card.

Ask Max on Mobile App

  1. When Max finds an offer you like, tap “Log in to link offers”. You’ll be guided to log into the provider’s site within the in-app browser.
  2. Once logged in, you can tap to detect offers on the page. If offers are available, Max will display the count.
  3. Tap again to start collecting — Max will go through offers one by one to link all of them to the account or card.
