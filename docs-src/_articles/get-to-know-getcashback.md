# Welcome to GetCashback.ai

**GetCashback.ai** is your AI-powered cashback copilot that helps you **discover**, **activate**, and **maximize** cashback offers across thousands of merchants, and leading credit cards, debit cards, and grocery loyalty programs—so you never miss a chance to earn, online or in-store.

## What is GetCashback.ai?

GetCashback.ai is available as a **browser extension**, **web platform**, and **mobile app** for iOS and Android. It brings together the power of AI and automation to help you:

- **Discover 5,000+ cashback offers** from 10+ cashback and reward programs  
- **Auto-activate cashback and clip digital coupons**, linking them directly to your card and grocer accounts so you never miss savings at checkout  
- **Track your cashback earnings** across all your programs in one dashboard  
- **Chat with Max**, your AI assistant, to find the best deals and offers effortlessly  
- **Get smart reminders and recommendations** powered by AI that understands offer terms and nudges you to use the best card at the right time  
- **Keep full control of your data** with privacy-first design, local storage by default, and optional Google Sheets export for transparency and control  

## Key Features

### 🔍 Smart Offer Discovery  
Our AI scans thousands of cashback offers in real-time and matches them with your intent—whether you're shopping online, in-store, or even just browsing.

### ⚡ Auto-Activation & Grocery Coupon Clipping  
GetCashback.ai now **automatically activates offers and clips digital coupons**, and links them to your **bank and grocer accounts**, so you're always saving without needing to remember.

### 💬 Ask Max: Your Cashback Copilot  
Ask Max anything—like “Where can I save on coffee?” or “Which card gives the best cashback at Target?” Max will search and summarize the best offers instantly.

### 📱 Mobile App (iOS & Android)  
Maximize cashback on the go with our mobile app. Browse, chat with Max, link and activate offers straight from your phone.

### 💳 Multi-Program & Card Support  
Track and manage offers from your credit cards, debit cards, and grocery loyalty programs.

### 📊 Earnings Dashboard  
Visualize your cashback history, top merchants, and programs you’re earning from. Make smarter spending decisions with insights powered by AI.

### 🎯 AI-Powered Recommendations & Reminders  
Our AI doesn't just match—you get proactive suggestions based on your browsing or purchase intent. Whether it’s choosing the right card, stacking offers, or spotting exclusions in the fine print, Max has your back.

### 🔒 Privacy & Data Control  
Your data is stored **locally by default**. If you want to export your offer usage or history, you can optionally sync to a **private Google Sheet**—nothing is shared without your consent.
