## Privacy and Security

### What Max Knows
- Messages you send to **Max**
- **Max**’s messages to you
- Active cashback offers from supported provider sources

### What Max Does Not Know
- Who you are
- Your email
- Your account information
- Your usernames or passwords
- Your transaction history
- Which offers are linked to your account

## Tips for Getting the Best Help

- **Be Specific**  
  Instead of “Show me grocery offers” try:  
  “Show me Safeway food offers for $20 purchases.”

- **Provide Context**  
  Instead of “What’s the best offer?” try:  
  “What’s the best offer for $500/month groceries and $300/month gas?”

- **Ask Follow-up Questions**  
  You can request clarifications, step-by-step guides, or reasoning behind recommendations.

If **Max** seems confused or off-track, tap the **“↻” Refresh** button in the top-right corner to reset the conversation. This clears the chat history and gives you a fresh start.

---

## Limitations

**Max** cannot:
- Make purchases or complete transactions
- Access your bank, card, or loyalty accounts directly
- Provide financial advice beyond cashback optimization
- Guarantee specific cashback amounts or outcomes
