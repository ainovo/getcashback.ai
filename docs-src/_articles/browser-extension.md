The GetCashback.ai browser extension is your AI-powered cashback copilot. It automatically collects, activates, and organizes offers while helping you make smarter cashback decisions — privately, securely, and effortlessly.

GetCashback.ai is available for:

- Google Chrome 
- Microsoft Edge
- Other Chromium-based browsers

## Register Your Extension

After installation:

1. Click the GetCashback.ai icon ![]({{ '/images/icon16.png' | relative_url }}) in your toolbar to open the Wallet.
2. You'll be prompted to register the extension.
3. Review the agreement, enter your email, and click **"I Agree"**.
4. Check your inbox for an email from **GetCashback.ai \<support@getcashback.ai\>** and click **"Verify Email Address"** button.
5. You’ll receive a welcome email confirming your registration.

Once registered, you’re ready to start collecting cashback offers automatically.

## Key Features

### 🧠 Auto-Collect Offers as You Browse

GetCashback.ai detects and auto-activates offers when you're logged into **supported banks or grocery loyalty websites**.

<img src="{{ '/images/automatically collect cashback offers.png' | relative_url }}" style="width: 80%;" alt="Automatically Collect Cashback Offers">

To explore supported sites:
- Open the Wallet and click **“Collect”** from the left menu.

Need a head start?
- On the **“Places to Hunt for Offers”** page, click **“Hunt Offers”** to be taken directly to the right offer pages.

When you arrive on a supported page:
- The **Widget** will appear and guide you through collecting.
- You can move the Widget by hoving over the icon and click **"Click here to move"** button if needed.
- Once the offer page fully loads, it will display the number of offers available.
- Click the Widget to start collecting. You’ll see progress and can pause/resume anytime.
- When done, click the extension icon to view collected offers in your Wallet.

### 🔒 Privacy-First by Design

We never access your bank credentials or share your data.

- No card linking or login sharing
- No browsing or purchase tracking
- Everything runs locally in your browser

#### Permissions Requested:
- **Active tab** – Detect merchant and bank pages
- **Storage** – Save preferences and offers locally
- **Notifications** – Remind you when offers are available
- **Geolocation** – Show in-store cashback nearby
- **Host permissions** – Display the auto-collect Widget
- **Identity** – *(Optional)* Upload offers to Google Sheets

### 📊 Smart Offer Discovery & Filtering

Your Wallet shows all collected offers with powerful tools to search, sort, and filter:

- Search by brand, product, or category
- Sort by expiration, value, or effort
- Filter by provider, card, category, type, redeeming location, status, min spend, and max cashback. 

<img src="{{ '/images/search and compare.png' | relative_url }}" style="width: 80%;" alt="Search and Compare">

### 🤖 AI Offer Review and Value Analysis

Many offers have unclear terms — we use AI to make them easy to understand.

- Standardized cashback, min spend, max cashback, expiration
- Extract redemption links
- Show **Value vs. Effort Ratings**
- Recommend best offers based on your spend patterns

<img src="{{ '/images/read fine print spot best deals.png' | relative_url }}" style="width: 80%;" alt="Read Fine Print Spot Best Deals">
